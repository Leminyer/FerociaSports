1. Who we are

FEROCIA Sports Center is a pickleball club in Boca Raton, Florida, operated by Ferocia Sports Corp. ("FEROCIA", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through ferociasports.com, the FEROCIA Player Portal (players.ferociasports.com), our emails and our online store, and what we do with it.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Player Portal account: your email address, first and last name, date of birth, mobile phone, gender and playing level. If you contact us from the portal's Help page, we keep your message.
  • Newsletter sign-up (FEROCIA Monthly): your name, email, date of birth, gender, mobile phone (optional), city and state.
  • Polls and RSVPs: the answer you choose when you reply to a poll we send by email.
  • Store orders: the items you buy, and the name, email and shipping address you enter at checkout.

Player DNA, Play and games

  • Your Player DNA: play style, side, formats, how you compete, when and where you like to play, what you're looking for right now, and which parts of it other players may see.
  • Recognitions you give and receive after ladder sessions, tournaments and games.
  • Games you create, join, are invited to, leave or cancel — including day, time, place, notes, the reasons you give and the reminders you choose.
  • Game results: who played, who didn't show up or cancelled late (and whether the host marked it as excused), the teams and the scores, and whether each player accepted or declined the result, with the reason they give.
  • Logged games and player codes: your player code, the codes you look up (so we can limit lookups), the players you add with their code, and the names of guests you add (people who aren't FEROCIA members). A guest's name is used only for that game's result.
  • Your Home: which option you pick under "What brings you in today?" (for example, "Plan a game"), so we can mark the one you use most as "Your usual". We keep each choice for 90 days and use it only for your Home. The charts on your Home are made from your own games; "Share my month" creates the image on your device and doesn't send it anywhere.
  • Players you block, and reports you send or that are made about you.
  • Notifications we send you in the portal, and whether you've read them.

Information the club records

  • Your player record: your ladders, matches, scores, standings, tournament results and the date you joined.
  • Club operations notes: for example, a record of changes to your player status, or a report if there is an incident at the club.
  • Email delivery status: whether an email we sent you was delivered or bounced, so we can fix bad addresses.

Information collected automatically

  • How you found us: if you arrive from a campaign link (for example an Instagram ad or a flyer QR code), we note the campaign source and the first page you landed on. This contains no name, email or other personal details. If you then subscribe, the source is saved with your subscription.
  • Technical information: like every website, the services that host our pages and data receive your IP address and basic browser information when your device connects to them.

We do not collect or store payment card numbers — payments are handled entirely by Stripe (see section 5).

3. How we use it

  • To run ladders, tournaments, clinics and other club activities, and to keep standings and results.
  • To create your Player Portal account and connect it to your existing player record. To do this we compare the details you enter (name, date of birth, email, phone) with our records. When the match isn't clear, a staff member reviews it before your account is linked.
  • To send you sign-in codes and links, and messages about your account.
  • To send FEROCIA Monthly and event news, if you subscribed.
  • To process and ship store orders.
  • To answer your questions and requests.
  • To keep the club, the website and our members safe — for example, to stop automated sign-up abuse.
  • To understand which campaigns bring people to the club, in summary form.
  • To suggest players you may enjoy playing with and games that fit you ("Play"). Suggestions are made automatically from your Player DNA, your level, when you play, your games at the club and the games you share with other players, and each one shows the reasons behind it. To order suggestions we may also use, without ever showing them: the coach rating our staff gives, attendance and cancellation history (only the last 6 months), and the recognitions you have given.
  • To run games: share game details with the players in them, send requests, invitations, answers, cancellations and reminders, and keep track of who joined.
  • To record game results: share a reported result with the players in that game so they can accept or decline it, and, once it's verified, show it in your own Play games record and count attendance, no-shows and late cancellations in your reliability. Play game results never change ladder standings.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, apply blocks, and limit or suspend accounts that break our rules.

When you update your phone number or email in the portal, we also update the same details on your newsletter subscription, so you only have to change them once.

4. What is public

Part of the fun of club play is seeing the standings. These pages on ferociasports.com are public and can be seen by anyone:

  • Ladder Hub: player names, rankings, matches, scores and ladder results.
  • Tournament results: player and team names, brackets, scores and finishes.
  • Photos and videos of club events that we choose to publish.

Your email, phone number, date of birth and other contact details are never shown on public pages. Your Player Portal shows your private details only to you, after you sign in. If you would prefer your name not to appear publicly, contact us and we will work with you.

5. What other players see

Inside the Player Portal, other players can see you only if you take part in Play, and only as your privacy settings allow:

  • Private: you don't appear in Play or games, and you can't see other players there either.
  • Limited: your first name and last initial, playing level, city, the parts of your Player DNA you choose to show, and how many recognitions you've received (never who gave them).
  • Public: the same, plus your full last name and your FEROCIA history — and your stats and recent games only if you turn them on.
  • In a game: the host sees the player card of anyone who asks to join, and the players in a game see each other's names and the game details.
  • Game results: the players in a game see that game's result — who played, the teams and the scores. Your Play games record (games, wins, win rate, streak) is shown only to you. Only the host who reported a result sees who accepted or declined it and why; FEROCIA staff see it too when they review a result.
  • Player codes: your code is shown only to you. Someone who has your code can find your first name, last initial and level to add you to a game they log; you then get the result to accept or decline. You can get a new code at any time (once a day), and the old one stops working.
  • Game chat: only the host and the players in a game can read its chat. FEROCIA staff don't read game chats; we only see a message if a player reports it, and we keep a copy of that message with the report.
  • Never shown to other players: your email, phone, date of birth, coach rating, reliability, who you blocked or reported, and who gave you a recognition.

You can change your privacy in My Identity ("Who can find you") and what your Player DNA shows in My DNA ("Who sees what") at any time.

7. Emails from us

  • Account emails (sign-in codes, account review results, replies to your help requests) are needed to use the Player Portal.
  • FEROCIA Monthly and event news are sent only if you subscribed and confirmed your email. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link, and you can leave at any time.
  • We don't send text messages (SMS).
  • Game emails (requests, invitations, answers, cancellations and reminders) are sent when you use Play and games.

8. Browser storage and cookies

We don't use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers. Our pages do save a few small things in your browser:

  • Player Portal sign-in: keeps you signed in on that device until you sign out.
  • Store cart: remembers the items in your cart.
  • Campaign source: remembers how you first found us, for 30 days, with no personal details (see section 2).

Some providers may set their own cookies: Cloudflare Turnstile, to tell people from bots; YouTube, when you play an embedded video; and Stripe, on its checkout page. You can clear browser storage and cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you do, you'll be signed out of the portal and your cart will be emptied.

9. How we protect it

Player Portal sign-in uses one-time codes and links sent to your email, so there is no password to steal. Each player can see only their own private details. Our database only lets club staff see member information, and every connection is encrypted (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly. If you think your account has been misused, contact us right away.

10. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you are part of the club and as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy. Match results and standings are kept as part of the club's history, because they also belong to the other players' records. Store order records are kept as long as needed for accounting and tax purposes. When we no longer need information, we delete it or remove the details that identify you.

Games, their details and their results are kept for up to 2 years so they can count in your history and stats. Game chat messages are deleted 30 days after the game (a reported message is kept with its report). Reports and the actions we took are kept for as long as needed to keep the community safe and to handle legal claims, normally up to 3 years.

11. Your choices and rights

  • See and update: you can see your details in the Player Portal and change your phone, city, gender, playing level, privacy and Player DNA there. To correct your name or date of birth, use Request a change on the portal or contact us.
  • Get a copy: ask us for a copy of the personal information we have about you.
  • Delete: ask us to close your portal account or delete your information. We'll delete what we're not required to keep. Past results that appear in other players' standings may stay as part of the club's history.
  • Stop newsletters: use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter.

Email contact@ferociasports.com from the address on your account and we'll answer within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first.

12. Age

The Player Portal is for players 18 and older. Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information online, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the club and our services change. We'll change the "Last updated" date at the top, and if a change is significant we'll let you know by email or in the Player Portal.
