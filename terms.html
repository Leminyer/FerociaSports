  • Who can use it. Only adults with a Player Portal account that is linked to their player record, who have completed their Player DNA and whose privacy is set to Limited or Public. We may require a membership to use these features in the future.
  • Suggestions are only suggestions. Recommendations and labels such as "Great match" are based on information players give us and on their games at the club. They are not a promise about anyone's skill, behavior, reliability or identity, and they are not an endorsement by FEROCIA.
  • Games are arranged between players. Unless we clearly say a game is a FEROCIA event, games created in the portal are organized by players, not by FEROCIA. We don't supervise them, staff them, insure them or check the place where they happen. FEROCIA does not book courts, collect court fees or split payments — players arrange those themselves.
  • Hosts. If you create a game, the details you give (day, time, place, level, notes) must be true, and you're responsible for having permission to play at the place you choose and for following its rules. You may host one active game at a time.
  • Joining and leaving. Asking to join is a request; the host decides. If you can't make it, leave the game or cancel as early as you can. Cancellations, late cancellations and no-shows may be recorded and may affect what we suggest to you and to others.

A result is verified when a player from the other team accepts it, or automatically if nobody declines within 48 hours. We may review, correct or discard any result, and reporting false results may lead to suspension from Play and games. Results of player games never change ladder standings or tournament results.
  • Keep it about the game. Game notes and anything you write must not include phone numbers, emails, social media handles or links, and must not be used to sell anything or promote events or businesses outside FEROCIA.
  • We can step in. We may edit or remove a game or any content, limit the number of games, invitations or requests, and suspend anyone from Play and games, at any time, to keep the community safe and fair.
